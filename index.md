# Bark in the Yard

> Stuntwoodriders in motion. Artistic and autistic verbalists. All condition eclectics. Advanced sociologic researchers. Allergic to the status quo and disposable antifuck. Sometimes we think. Sometimes we don't.

**Future, power, primitive, raw.**

Bark in the Yard is an international art collective working across contemporary visual art, urban culture, critical practice and collective action. Including posters, photography, video, zines, sound-image work, participatory projects, digital culture and practices that do not yet have a settled name, increasingly crossing disciplinary boundaries and engaging technology, social issues and cultural context.

## Positioning

Exploring the perceived geometrical and emotional relationships between humans and a given subsonic content. With the emphasis on the conscious and subliminal effects upon the individual. The collective explores notions of concealment, the dichotomy between interior and exterior spaces, and the act of looking in a media-saturated environment. Its work establishes an open-ended dialogue about social and generational identity amidst shifting visual narratives of the urban environment, counterculture movements and contemporary lifestyles.

It investigates the understanding of a globally connected reality and the interpretative frameworks through which people think about, experience, evaluate and decide to act in places, spaces and communities.
