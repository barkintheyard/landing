# Bark in the Yard

> Stuntwoodriders in motion. Artistic and autistic verbalists. All-condition eclectics. Advanced sociologic researchers. Allergic to the status quo. Sometimes we think. Sometimes we don't.

**Future, power, primitive, raw.**

Bark in the Yard is an international collective working across contemporary visual culture, urban art, critical practice and collective action. It also works across zines, posters, photography, video, sound-image work, participatory projects, digital culture and practices that do not yet have a settled name, increasingly crossing disciplinary boundaries and engaging technology, social issues and cultural context.

## Positioning

The collective explores notions of concealment, the dichotomy between interior and exterior spaces, and the act of looking in a media-saturated environment. Its work establishes an open-ended dialogue about social and generational identity amidst shifting visual narratives of the urban environment, counterculture movements and contemporary lifestyles.

It investigates the understanding of a globally connected reality and the interpretative frameworks through which people think about, experience, evaluate and decide to act in places, spaces and communities.
