# Selected commissions and brand clients

Bark in the Yard has provided strategy, branding, visual communication and urban art curation for cultural organisations, exhibitions, festivals and boardsport companies, to name but a few.

## Selected commissions

- 2021–2023 · House of Digital Art — Port Louis, Mauritius. Strategy, branding and visual communication.
- 2018–2021 · Réunion Métis, Festival of Contemporary Creation — Saint-Paul, La Réunion. Strategy, visual communication and urban art curation.
- 2013–2017 · Porlwi, Festival of Contemporary Culture — Port Louis, Mauritius. Strategy, branding, visual communication and urban art curation.
- 2009–2012 · Run Vie, Festival of Urban Art & Music — Vienna, Austria. Branding, visual communication and urban art curation.
